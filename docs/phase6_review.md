## Limits that must remain in the paper

- Source-max conformal calibration gives marginal control under exchangeable
  sources in a represented photographic collection and a fixed query/view
  family. It does not guarantee each realized split or arbitrary new shifts.
- Source independence, semantic deduplication, exchangeability and historical
  pretraining exposure are assumptions or separate data checks, not facts
  established by hashes. Source-bootstrap intervals condition on the fitted
  models and calibration and remain approximate.
- The pooled zero-harm bound requires zero harmful source events and source
  independence at fixed quotas. Zero events among 200 sources per family do
  not certify a simultaneous 1% family margin. Nonzero-harm bootstrap support
  must not be described as an exact finite-sample certificate.
- Common resolution/encoding does not eliminate semantic or collection cues
  between GenImage and photographic collections. These inputs are not caption
  paired. Secondary multi-model and subgroup comparisons are descriptive.
- Native payload recovery and photographic margin calibration do not
  authenticate an issuer or guarantee null rates on unmarked AI or wrong
  payloads. Received-image JPEG90 evidence is deployment available, but its
  calibration is still specific to the registered processing family.
- Core cuts on the nine extra conditions are diagnostics outside their
  calibration family. Separately calibrated attack-family cuts remain
  secondary. Reconstructed photographs have input-source provenance labels
  and contain generated pixels. Equal pixel PSNR does not establish equal
  semantic quality; residual-budget adaptations differ from native attacks.
- Abstention and risk-coverage are descriptive and provide no selective-risk
  or shift-detection guarantee. The review does not establish conference
  acceptance or algorithmic novelty.
